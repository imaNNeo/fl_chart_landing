Download​

I suggest you to download the FL Chart Example App to see how FL Chart works in a real app. This way, you can see the charts in action and understand how to use them in your own projects. Also, the native app is a great way to test the performance of FL Chart on your device. At the moment, FL Chart Example App is available on Google Play and App Store. Other stores will be added in the future. (Don't forget to rate the app!)

You can also open the app in your browser here: FL Chart Example App.
